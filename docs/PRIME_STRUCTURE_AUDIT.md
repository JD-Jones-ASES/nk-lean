# Prime structure formalization — completed

All source modules are in `/private/tmp/nk-lean-work/NK/`. They were proved in
Lean 4.33.0 using the existing pinned Mathlib/RK dependencies. No custom axiom,
`sorry`, `native_decide`, or unchecked oracle appears in the new sources.

## UnitConductor.lean

`unitConductor p k` is `vp(k)+1` for odd primes, `1` for p=2 and odd k,
and `v2(k)+2` for p=2 and even k. The main theorem
`unitPower_stabilizes` proves, for every prime p, k≥1, p∤u and n≥s(p,k),

    (exists z : Nat, z^k % p^n = u % p^n)
      iff (exists z : Nat, z^k % p^s = u % p^s).

Coverage includes every singular odd prime and every even binary exponent.
The cyclic odd-unit case follows by equality of power-image indices. The
binary proof shows the kernel of units reduction modulo 4 is cyclic of order
2^(n−2), generated by 5, and normalizes even-power roots by changing their sign.

`unitConductor_le_exponent_iff` proves s(p,k)≤k iff (p,k)≠(2,2), under
p prime and k≥1. `unitPower_stabilizes_block` gives stabilization already at
p^k outside that pair. The explicit binary-square counterexample is 5:
it is a square modulo 4 and is not a square modulo 16.
The conductor is a sufficient precision; no universal minimality is claimed.

## PrimeBlockLex.lean

`powerArc_iff_firstDifference_block` proves exact arc equivalence between the
full graph modulo (p^k)^e and first-difference words of e low-to-high base-p^k
blocks, for p prime, k,e≥1, and (p,k)≠(2,2). The reverse implication handles
nonunit differences: the common lower blocks are factored out and the residual
unit is lifted with the conductor theorem.

`blockWordEquiv b e hb` is the actual digit bijection

    Fin (b^e) ≃ (Fin e → Fin b), for b≥2,

including leading zero blocks and e=0. `powerArc_iff_blockWord` shows that this
bijection preserves and reflects every arc in the stated prime domain.
`binary_square_block_graph_counterexample` proves that the first base-four
block admits 0→5 while the full graph modulo 16 rejects that arc.

## PrimeBlockCapacity.lean

`modularCapacity k m f` is the ordinary interval-moment supremum for the full
modular kth-power digraph on Fin m. It permits arbitrary selected supports and
arbitrary admissible intervals. `prime_power_capacity` proves

    modularCapacity k (p^(k*e)) f = (modularCapacity k (p^k) f)^e

for p prime, k,e≥1, f≥0, outside (p,k)=(2,2). This uses the proved graph
isomorphism and certificate_audit's independently formalized ordinary
lexicographic capacity theorem; no recursive capacity law is assumed.

`IntervalAlphabet.toPowerDigraph_moment` proves exact preservation of the
moment when converting the project's existing IntervalAlphabet record.
`IntervalAlphabet.moment_le_prime_power_capacity` exposes the full depth
barrier directly on that original interface.

The law is for depths n=k*e. It does not claim this same formula for arbitrary
partial-block exponents, for coupled distinct primes, for negative f, or for
binary squares. It makes no numerical optimality claim for the one-block
capacity itself.
